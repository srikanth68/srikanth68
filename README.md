## `01` — Who I Am

> _I believe great software isn't just built — it's **architected**. My work sits at the intersection of clean engineering, thoughtful design, and forward-looking tech._

I started out as a teaching assistant, explaining code to eager students. Somewhere along the way I fell in love with the art of **structuring systems** — not just writing code, but designing the blueprints that make teams ship faster and software last longer.

Over **14+ years** I've worn many hats — developer, analyst, senior engineer, architect — all within the **.NET ecosystem**. Today I lead architectural decisions for the **State of Maine's Office of IT** while staying curious about emerging tech like generative AI and how it can sharpen the way we design and deliver software.
